Privacy policy for AI Privacy Guard

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Contact: thebongobongo228@gmail.com

AI Privacy Guard is a Chrome extension that looks for sensitive information in forms and Enter-to-send chat boxes before you send them, and that can show you what your browser is about to send to other sites. This page explains what that means for your privacy, in plain language.

We do not collect your data

The extension does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not send your browsing data, page content, form values, chat text, or request history to the developer or to anyone else. There is no analytics service, no crash-reporting service, no advertising network, and no backend server for this product.

The extension does not contact AI companies. A list of known AI destinations is bundled inside the extension so it can recognise them locally.

Everything runs on your device

Detection and request observation happen in your browser. If the extension warns you about a secret, that decision is made on your computer. Other people, including the developer, cannot see what you typed or where you browsed because of this extension.

What is stored locally, and for how long

On your computer, the extension may store:

  • A short activity log of outgoing requests it could see: destination, time, method, category (for example “AI service” or “the site you are on”), whether anything looked sensitive, and whether a rule you created allowed, warned about, or stopped it.
  • Masked samples of detections (for example that an email address was found), not the full secret.
  • Rules and settings you choose, such as “warn me before sending a form” or “forget activity after 24 hours.”
  • A local error log used only so you can copy or save a report if something breaks. That report is built on your device and is not uploaded unless you choose to send it yourself.

Activity is kept for 24 hours by default. You can change that retention window in Settings. Older records are deleted automatically on your device.

The extension does not store raw request bodies, response bodies, passwords, login header values, or the full text you typed into a form or chat box.

How to erase what it stored

  • Use Clear activity in the dashboard to delete the local request log.
  • Use the reset controls in Settings to return settings and rules to their defaults.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage from Chrome.

Permissions, in plain words

Chrome will show that the extension can access data on all websites, watch network requests, apply rules you create, show notifications, and save data on your device. Those permissions exist so it can warn you on the page you are using and show you what that page is sending. They are not used to upload that information.

Some limits are permanent: the extension cannot read a website’s replies, cannot read messages inside an already-open live connection, cannot see a site forward your words from its own servers to an AI provider, and today it only intercepts native form submits and Enter-to-send chats — not paste and not a Send-button click.

Support and tip links

The extension can show a Support button and an optional tip page, plus links to social profiles and an email address. Those links open a normal web page in a new tab only when you click them. Clicking them is optional. Nothing in the product is locked behind a tip or a follow. The extension itself does not load those sites in the background.
